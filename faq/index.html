Church Cleaning FAQs for Scandia Businesses

Common questions from church administrators, facilities coordinators and trustee boards before and after signing a cleaning contract.

How do you handle access to a locked building after hours?
We work with your facilities coordinator to establish a documented key or code arrangement. Access credentials are tracked internally, and the assigned crew lead is the only person who holds them. When a contract ends, access is returned or changed within one business day.
Can you clean around a mid-week service schedule?
Yes. We build your event calendar into the cleaning schedule before the contract starts. If your congregation runs a Wednesday evening service, a Thursday morning Bible study and a Friday youth group, we account for all of it. Schedule changes are communicated to us — we track them and adjust.
What happens if the crew is sick or has a vehicle problem?
We notify your facilities contact before your building opens and arrange a makeup clean the same day or next day, depending on your schedule. We do not leave you to discover the problem. This is part of what 22 years of operations looks like in practice.
Do you use fragrance-free or low-chemical products?
Available on request. A number of the congregations we serve have members with fragrance sensitivities, asthma or chemical allergies. We can specify the products used in your scope of work and switch to fragrance-free or Green Seal-certified alternatives where needed.
Can we get a same-week start?
Occasionally yes, depending on crew availability in your area. Realistically, most new contracts start within two to three weeks of signing — we need time to do the walkthrough, finalize the scope and assign a crew. Rush starts are the exception, not the rule.
Who do I call if I have a problem with a clean?
Your crew lead, whose direct contact is in the scope-of-work document. If you cannot reach them, call the main office at (866) 958-8773 during business hours (Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm). Email to ops@thequotemasters.com is also monitored daily.
Do you offer one-time cleaning for events?
Yes — post-wedding, post-funeral, post-holiday service cleaning is available on a per-event basis. The per-visit rate for one-time events is higher than contracted weekly cleaning, but it is available. Call or use the quote form to request a one-time clean.
What is the minimum contract term?
We typically ask for a three-month initial term, which covers the walkthrough, scope finalization and the first-month check-in. After the initial term, contracts are month-to-month with 30 days written notice to cancel on either side.
