Sanctuary & Worship Space Cleaning in Hugo, MN

Sanctuary cleaning for faith facilities in Hugo and the surrounding area — scheduled around your service calendar, priced transparently, documented in writing.

Congregations in Hugo share the same cleaning challenge as faith communities across Washington and Chisago Counties: the sanctuary needs to look and feel cared for every Sunday, regardless of what happened in the building the week before. Weddings, funerals, special services and holiday events all leave additional cleaning demand. A professional cleaning schedule built around your calendar handles that demand without relying on the same volunteers every week.

Example facility: A 3,200-square-foot growing-congregation sanctuary with padded chairs rather than fixed pews — flexible layout for Saturday evening and Sunday morning services requires reset between each service in addition to the regular weekly clean.

Scandia Church Cleaning has spent 22 years scheduling around exactly this kind of complexity. We assess the facility in person or from a floor plan, agree on scope in writing, and assign the same crew to your building each visit.

What Is Included

  • Pew or chair cleaning (wood surfaces wiped, cushions vacuumed)
  • Carpet vacuuming throughout nave and choir areas
  • Altar, chancel and pulpit surfaces wiped
  • Aisle runners vacuumed and repositioned
  • Entry vestibule swept and mopped
  • Restrooms serving the sanctuary cleaned and restocked

Quarterly deep-clean visits add: hard floor stripping and waxing, pew cushion extraction, light fixture dusting.
