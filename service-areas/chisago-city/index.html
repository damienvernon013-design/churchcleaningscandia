Church Cleaning in Chisago City, MN

Faith facility cleaning for congregations in Chisago City and the surrounding area — 22 years of reliable, documented service.

Chisago City has a population of around 5,500 and sits approximately 18 miles north of Scandia — about 25 minutes via Highway 8 from our Scandia base. Congregations here range from historic established churches to newer growing communities, and the cleaning needs reflect the full range of faith facility use: regular Sunday services, mid-week programs, community events and seasonal occasions.

About Chisago City

  • Chisago City is the county seat of Chisago County and part of the Chisago Lakes area, a cluster of small cities — including Lindstrom, Center City and Shafer — that share a common Scandinavian settlement heritage and a high density of historic Lutheran congregations.
  • The Chisago Lakes community observes the Chisago County Fair in August, a major regional event that redirects volunteer labor and creates temporary gaps in church operational staffing.
  • Chisago City sits on a chain of four connected lakes — Green, Chisago, North and South — whose shoreline communities include a number of faith facilities that serve seasonal cabin populations in addition to year-round residents.
