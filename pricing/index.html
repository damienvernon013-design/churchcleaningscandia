Church Cleaning Pricing in Scandia, MN

Real local price ranges for faith facility cleaning. Transparent, no hidden fees, fixed-price contracts.

Most church cleaning services do not publish pricing. We do — because a trustee board reviewing a cleaning proposal deserves to know whether a quote is reasonable before the first phone call, and because price transparency is the fastest way to build trust with a committee that has been through several contractors.

The ranges below reflect market rates in the Scandia, Washington County and Chisago County area as of our most recent pricing review. Your exact quote will depend on square footage, number of spaces, frequency and access requirements.

Bundled Pricing

Facilities that contract for multiple spaces (sanctuary + fellowship hall, for example) receive a bundled rate that is typically 10–15% lower than the sum of individual-space quotes. We manage one contract, one invoice and one point of contact — that efficiency flows back to the price.

Frequency Discount

Weekly cleaning carries a lower per-visit rate than bi-weekly or monthly, because predictable scheduling reduces our logistics cost. The more consistent your schedule, the better your per-visit price.

What Is Not Included

Standard pricing does not include: exterior window cleaning, roof or gutter clearing, carpet extraction cleaning (available as a quarterly add-on), pressure washing, or restoration-level cleaning after significant events. These are quoted separately on request.
