Nursery & Classroom Sanitizing in Scandia, MN

Children's ministry spaces carry a higher hygiene obligation. We clean and sanitize to the standard parents expect — and that your congregation's insurance requires.

Nurseries and Sunday school classrooms are the rooms that parents inspect without being asked. Sticky toys, soiled carpet and poorly cleaned restrooms are the fastest way to lose a young family's confidence in a congregation — and to catch illness spreading through an entire age group in two Sundays.

Scandia Church Cleaning has spent 22 years cleaning children's ministry spaces in rural Minnesota. We understand the difference between cleaning a classroom for adults and sanitizing a space where toddlers put things in their mouths.

What Nursery & Classroom Cleaning Includes

  • Hard toy sanitizing using an approved disinfectant safe for children's items
  • Soft toys spot-cleaned and aired; flagged for laundering when needed
  • Carpet vacuumed thoroughly including under furniture
  • Hard floors swept and mopped with child-safe disinfectant
  • Tables and chairs wiped and sanitized
  • Changing tables cleaned and disinfected
  • Restrooms and diaper stations cleaned and restocked
  • Trash removed and bins relined
  • Window sills and low surfaces wiped

Frequency

  • Weekly — standard for any active nursery or Sunday school program
  • After illness incidents — additional deep-clean available within 24 hours on request
  • Seasonal deep-clean — quarterly sanitizing of all soft furnishings and stored materials

Products and Safety

We use disinfectants that are EPA-registered and approved for use in children's environments. Specific products are disclosed on request for any congregation with fragrance or chemical sensitivities — common in communities that include members with asthma or allergies.
