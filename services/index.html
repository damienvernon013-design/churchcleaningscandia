Our Church Cleaning Services in Scandia

Three specialized cleaning programs for faith facilities — each scoped to the space, the schedule and the congregation it serves.

Scandia Church Cleaning has been cleaning faith facilities across Minnesota for 22 years. Our services are structured around the three distinct spaces in a typical church building, because each one has a different cleaning standard, a different schedule pressure and a different audience to satisfy.

Sanctuary & Worship Space Cleaning

Pews, carpet, altar, chancel surfaces, aisle runners and choir areas. Scheduled ahead of Sunday services and around mid-week events. The standard here is reverence — the space should feel cared for, not just dusted.

Full details →

Fellowship Hall & Kitchen Cleaning

Tables, chairs, hard floors, kitchen surfaces and equipment. Designed for multi-use spaces that turn over multiple times a week between meals, meetings and events. Odor control and grease management are the operational priorities.

Full details →

Nursery & Classroom Sanitizing

Toy sanitizing, soft surface care, floor disinfection, changing table care and restroom cleaning. Child-safe disinfectants, disclosed on request. The hygiene standard here has to hold up to a parent's scrutiny.

Full details →

How Services Are Scoped

We start with a walkthrough or a floor-plan review, agree on the exact scope in writing, and give you a fixed-price quote. Nothing is added to the invoice after the fact. If your building has spaces that don't fit neatly into one of the three categories — a library, a gym, a commercial kitchen rented out to a caterer — we scope those individually.

Services can be bundled: a congregation that needs sanctuary and fellowship hall cleaning weekly gets one quote, one contract and one invoice. We don't require you to manage separate vendors for each space.

Scheduling

We work around your calendar. If your Sunday morning service starts at 9:00am and your Saturday afternoon wedding ends at 6:00pm, we clean from 7:00pm Saturday to ensure the building is ready. We track your events schedule and flag conflicts before they become problems.
