About Scandia Church Cleaning

22 years cleaning faith facilities in Minnesota. One consistent standard, one fixed price, one crew you can count on.

Scandia Church Cleaning has spent 22 years providing professional cleaning to faith communities across Washington and Chisago Counties, Minnesota. The congregations we work with range from small rural chapels to multi-building campuses. The common thread is not size — it is that they needed a cleaning crew who understood the rhythm of a working church.

What 22 Years in This Business Looks Like

After 22 years, the patterns are familiar. Volunteer cleaning teams burn out over two to three years. The person who managed the previous contractor retires and nobody documented the scope. A new trustee board inherits the building and the budget and doesn't know where to start. These are the situations we are called into, and we have learned to make the transition as simple as possible.

We write the scope of work. We price it transparently. We show up on schedule. When something changes — a new event series, a seasonal deep-clean requirement, a facility renovation — we adjust the contract in writing, not verbally over the phone.

Our Approach

We are a service-area business. We do not have a storefront or a fixed premises — we come to your facility. Our teams serve Scandia, Marine on Saint Croix, Forest Lake, Hugo, Chisago City, Lindstrom, Stillwater, May Township and Wyoming, Minnesota, all within 25 miles of our Scandia base.

Each facility gets a consistent assigned crew — not a rotating pool of strangers. The crew learns your building, your schedule and your preferences, and the relationship is managed by a named contact who answers the phone during business hours.

Credentials

We are fully insured and bonded. Documentation is available for your trustee board on request. See our credentials page for full detail on coverage levels and bonding.
