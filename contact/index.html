What to Have Ready

To give you an accurate quote as quickly as possible, it helps to have:

  • Approximate square footage of each space you want cleaned
  • A sense of your cleaning frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, event-based)
  • Your typical event schedule — Sundays only, or multiple events per week?
  • Any specific requirements (fragrance-free products, after-hours access, locked areas)

You do not need to have all of this ready to call us. We can work through it together.
