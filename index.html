Why a Trustee Board Chooses a Cleaning Contractor

Most churches that contact us are coming from one of three situations: a volunteer cleaning team that is burning out, a previous contractor who stopped showing up reliably, or a facility manager who inherited the job along with every other responsibility the congregation asked them to carry.

What they need is simple: a written scope of work, a consistent crew, and a phone number that gets answered. After 22 years, we have learned that the details that matter most to a trustee board are accountability and price transparency — not claims and promises.

We publish our local pricing, explain our process in full, and carry the insurance and bonding documentation your board will ask for. See our credentials page for specifics.
